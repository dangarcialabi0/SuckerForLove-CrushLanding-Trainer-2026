## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sucker for Love: Crush Landing**. This trainer provides optional assistance for exploring the apartment, solving puzzles, managing interactions, testing dialogue choices, and progressing through story routes without changing the game's core presentation.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to revisit story routes, experiment with choices, solve puzzles more conveniently, explore the apartment freely, or reduce repetition during multiple-ending playthroughs.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after entering the apartment and reaching a controllable exploration scene. Exploration assistance is intended for navigating the game's hand-drawn 360-degree apartment environment. Avoid toggling movement-related options during scene transitions or dialogue-only sequences.

### Tools & Inventory

The game focuses on examining objects, collecting clues, and using items to solve colorful puzzles. Use puzzle assistance only when needed, and disable it before loading another save to keep item and interaction states synchronized.

### World & Environment

The apartment is the central exploration space, filled with interactive objects, clues, story events, and supernatural disturbances connected to Hheily's arrival. Choice route control is useful for comparing consequences, discovering alternate scenes, and reaching the game's multiple endings.

### Combat & Weapons

Sucker for Love: Crush Landing does not use a traditional combat system. Dangerous visitors and supernatural events are handled through story scenes, puzzles, and interactions. Threat sequence protection is intended to provide convenience during those encounters without introducing attacks, weapons, or combat statistics.
